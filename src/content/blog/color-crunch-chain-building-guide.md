If your [Color Crunch](../../color-crunch) run starts strong and then falls apart, you are not alone. Most players lose control because they chase one big clear and forget to set up the next board state.

## Core Loop

Think in pairs: "this clear" and "the next setup."  
Pick a high-value cluster, clear it cleanly, and immediately scan for your follow-up color.  
The goal is to stay one move ahead, not scramble after the board gets messy.

## Mistakes To Avoid

1. Taking tiny clears just because they are easy, even when they break future chains.
2. Locking onto one color while the rest of the board is getting fragmented.
3. Refusing recovery moves because they feel "too defensive."

## Practice Routine

1. Play two warmup rounds focused only on keeping the board balanced.
2. In your next three rounds, say your next target color out loud before every move.
3. End with one review: find two moments where a safe setup would have outperformed a greedy clear.

## Target Milestones

1. Increase your average chain length across five sessions.
2. Reach late-round pressure without the board collapsing.
3. Need fewer emergency recovery moves by the end of each session.
