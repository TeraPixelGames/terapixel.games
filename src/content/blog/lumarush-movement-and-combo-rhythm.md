[LumaRush](../../lumarush) is a control game first and a speed game second. If your movement is stable, score follows.

## Core Loop

Read spawn pressure, pick a lane with escape options, then convert safe windows into combo pushes.  
The most reliable runs alternate between recovery and aggression instead of forcing full aggression the entire time.

## Mistakes To Avoid

1. Holding center lane too long when pressure stacks.
2. Burning panic dashes into unsafe space.
3. Forcing combos after a near miss instead of resetting.

## Practice Routine

1. First run: survival only, no combo greed.
2. Second run: add short combo bursts after every safe reset.
3. Third run: record where deaths happen and tag the trigger pattern.

## Target Milestones

1. Survive your opening minute with zero panic corrections.
2. Chain at least two clean combo windows per run.
3. End each session with one run that beats your baseline by 10%.
