In [SpeedSolitaire](../../speedsolitaire), most lost time is not bad luck. It is pause time. If you can keep your decisions flowing, your clear times drop fast.

## Core Loop

Scan the tableau first, prioritize reveal-heavy moves, and keep a steady rhythm.  
A smooth chain of good decisions beats occasional perfect moves separated by long pauses.

## Mistakes To Avoid

1. Cycling the same options without changing board state.
2. Delaying obvious reveal moves while hunting the perfect line.
3. Over-focusing one stack while the rest of the board stalls.

## Practice Routine

1. Warm up with a no-pause rule for your opening sequence.
2. Run one game focused only on reveal priority.
3. Final run: if you get stuck, reset scan order immediately instead of forcing.

## Target Milestones

1. Lower average completion time week over week.
2. Complete more runs without mid-game stalls.
3. Notice fewer "frozen" decision moments.
