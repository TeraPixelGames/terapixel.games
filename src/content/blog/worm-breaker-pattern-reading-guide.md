If [Worm Breaker](../../worm-breaker) feels chaotic, your reads are probably one beat too late. The game gets much calmer once you start predicting movement instead of reacting to it.

## Core Loop

Identify the active pattern, pre-position for the next beat, and keep one safe escape lane open.  
Late corrections are expensive, so early reads are your best defense.

## Mistakes To Avoid

1. Chasing short-term gains into tight, enclosed space.
2. Mirroring threat movement instead of taking safe angles.
3. Staying static while tempo changes around you.

## Practice Routine

1. Warm up with one run focused only on survival.
2. Do two runs where you call next threat direction before it lands.
3. Final run: after every aggressive move, confirm your escape setup.

## Target Milestones

1. Reduce deaths caused by edge traps.
2. Increase average run length over five sessions.
3. Build a repeatable open-safe-open movement rhythm.
