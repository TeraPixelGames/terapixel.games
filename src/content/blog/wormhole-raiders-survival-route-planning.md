In [Wormhole Raiders](../../wormhole-raiders), raw reflex helps, but route planning wins runs. If you enter pressure spikes without an exit plan, your run is already in danger.

## Core Loop

Every few seconds, ask two questions: where is my next safe zone, and what can block my exit?  
Planning one move ahead turns panic moments into manageable decisions.

## Mistakes To Avoid

1. Taking narrow routes with no backup exit.
2. Fixating on one threat while edge spawns stack up.
3. Overcorrecting after small mistakes and burning position.

## Practice Routine

1. First runs: only choose routes with two exits.
2. Mid-session: take aggressive lanes only when fallback is clear.
3. End session: review the final 20 seconds of your best and worst run.

## Target Milestones

1. Increase average survival time by 15% over one week.
2. Cut deaths caused by trapped movement.
3. Finish at least one run per session with zero panic reversals.
