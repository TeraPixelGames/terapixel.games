About Terapixel Games

Terapixel Games is a small browser-game publisher focused on original arcade, puzzle, and card games that run in modern web browsers. The site exists for players who want working games, clear instructions, honest release status, and a direct way to contact the studio when something needs attention.

What We Make

We publish lightweight web games with immediate controls, readable goals, and short repeatable runs. The current live library includes arcade movement, color-matching puzzle, and speed-focused card games exported for desktop and mobile browsers.

How We Label Releases

Public game pages have active launch links, fullscreen paths, and written instructions. Unreleased projects stay out of public navigation and the sitemap until they provide enough value for players.

What Players Can Expect

Every live game page should explain the core loop, controls, practical play tips, and what kind of player the game is for before asking anyone to launch fullscreen. If something breaks, the Support and Contact pages provide a direct email channel.

Editorial Standard

We keep site copy specific to games we operate, avoid fake download claims, and separate publisher information from ads, account tools, and shop experiments. The goal is a site that is useful with or without monetization.

Review and Maintenance

We review public pages for broken links, misleading labels, thin copy, and unclear calls to action before asking advertising partners to evaluate the site. Pages that are not ready for public discovery are kept out of the sitemap until they provide enough value for visitors.

Contact the Studio

For gameplay reports, privacy questions, or partnership inquiries, email support@terapixel.games or use the Support page.
