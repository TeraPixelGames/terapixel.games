Why These Games Exist

About Terapixel

Short Sessions, Clear Goals

Terapixel Games focuses on browser games that load quickly, explain their goals clearly, and give players a concrete reason to retry. The site is built around playable exports, direct support contact, and written guides that help players understand each game before or after launching it.

Playable Builds First

Live games stay paired with instructions, controls, tips, and guide notes so the page is useful even before a player opens the embedded build. Preview-only projects stay out of public navigation until they have a working browser export.
