Design notes

What to Watch

LumaRush is built around readable momentum. The useful skill is not only reacting quickly, but keeping enough control to avoid overcorrecting when the pace rises.

Player guide

LumaRush Strategy Notes

Who LumaRush Is For

LumaRush fits players who like short arcade runs where improvement is visible quickly. A good session is not about unlocking a long checklist; it is about reading the lane, reacting cleanly, and starting another run with a slightly better rhythm.

How to Build Consistency

The most reliable improvement comes from watching upcoming movement space instead of reacting at the last possible moment. Players who make small corrections early usually last longer than players who wait for a dramatic save.

When to Use Fullscreen

Fullscreen is recommended when the embedded frame feels tight or when browser chrome distracts from the play area. It gives the exported build more space and helps keep focus on motion cues.
