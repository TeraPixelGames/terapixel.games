Privacy Policy

Last updated: March 4, 2026

Overview

This Privacy Policy explains how Terapixel Games handles data when you visit terapixel.games, play browser games, and contact support.

Data Categories We Process

  • Technical usage data such as browser type, device category, page path, and timestamps
  • Diagnostics and error data used to maintain service availability
  • Support contact data that you choose to send by email

We do not require sensitive personal information to load or play publicly available games. If you contact support, we process only the information needed to respond to your request.

Cookies, Analytics, And Advertising

Terapixel Games uses analytics and advertising services that may set cookies or similar identifiers to measure site traffic, understand gameplay page usage, and support ad delivery. These services may include Google Analytics and Google AdSense.

Third-party providers may process data according to their own policies. You can control cookies through browser settings and privacy tools offered by your browser vendor.

How We Use Data

  • Operate and secure the website and game pages
  • Diagnose errors and improve performance
  • Respond to support requests
  • Measure traffic and ad performance at an aggregate level

Data Retention

We retain support communications and operational logs only as long as needed for support, compliance, and service reliability purposes. Retention windows may vary by provider.

Third-Party Services

Some game experiences are embedded or linked from third-party hosting platforms. When you interact with those services, their privacy terms and policies apply in addition to this policy.

Your Choices

You may request removal of support emails you sent to us, subject to legal and security obligations. You may also control cookies through your browser configuration.

Children's Privacy

This site is intended for a general audience and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Policy Updates

We may update this policy as the platform evolves. Updated versions are published on this page with a revised effective date.

Privacy Contact

For privacy questions or requests, email support@terapixel.games with "Privacy Request" in the subject line.
